A-2

The install sequence

Four stages · fixed scope
  1. 01

    Survey

    1 week

    We sit with the people doing the work and time it — the real process, including the parts nobody documented. You get a work order naming the workflows, their current cycle times, and a target.

  2. 02

    Fabricate

    2–4 weeks

    We build in your stack, against your real data, with your edge cases. No sandbox demo that quietly assumes clean inputs.

  3. 03

    Commission

    1 week

    It runs in parallel with the humans until the error rate holds inside the tolerance we agreed. Signed off against the work order — not just shipped.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    Ongoing

    Models drift, vendors change their APIs, and your process changes in March. We keep it in tolerance, and you can see every run it made.
